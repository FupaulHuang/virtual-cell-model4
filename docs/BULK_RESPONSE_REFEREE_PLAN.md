## What the implementation establishes

The frozen model definition (local artifact omitted: `../models/m4_multisource_dual/model.json`) records arithmetic-CPM amplitude 0.6, bulk amplitude 0.3, common-response subtraction 1.0, prior mass 100,000, and source weights K562/HCT116/HEK293T/H1/CD4 = 2/1/1/2/0.5. Its historical selection-status fields describe the freeze; the official receipt establishes the later published result.

In [model.py](../external/atlasshift/model.py), `load_xatlas` forms a control-shrunk raw-count probability. `aligned_effect` computes bulk response as `log1p(50000 * pert_probability) - log1p(50000 * control_probability)`. `desired_mean` applies the bulk amplitude in that same natural-log space, adds the destination bulk-control profile, applies `expm1`, and normalizes to a probability. The factor 50,000 is consistent in both directions. The [control template](../external/atlasshift/predict.py) obtains bulk control from pooled raw counts and arithmetic control from the average of per-cell normalized counts. The [builder](../scripts/build_prediction.py) passes the corresponding control to each head. No clear factor-of-scale, log-base, or mean-log-versus-log-mean bug was found in this path.

This is not proof that the transferred effects are biologically calibrated. Source native gene panels differ; K562 measures 8,246 unique genes, while Orion and H1 cover wider panels. Source bulk probabilities are normalized on their saved panels. CD4's bulk contribution is inferred from published LFCs and destination controls, not measured library-weighted pseudobulk. These are explicit transfer approximations. Renormalizing stored arithmetic means cannot reconstruct per-cell normalization on a different panel.

The actual bank metadata and builders were checked, rather than inferring units from filenames:

- **K562:** `K562_GWPS_CPM_full_statistics.npz` has `source=K562_GWPS_raw_subset`; its frozen receipt (local artifact omitted: `../data/K562_GWPS_CPM_full_statistics.receipt.json`) records SHA-256 `6afffc2e1ef26999f50845f2d885e35e465efac894bc54344ac5629f8ab2ffbe` and 1,121,484,815 observed counts over 96,111 selected cells. The [builder](../scripts/gwps_subset_bank.py) checks observed `X` values are finite nonnegative integers, aggregates them directly into `target_count_sums`, and derives batch library weights from those row sums. Arithmetic CPM is computed separately. No synthetic `source_depth` or assay-CPM-as-counts conversion is used in this frozen bank. Its library weights are observed **native-panel count totals**, not evidence of unobserved full-transcriptome UMI depths. The receipt verifies selected public byte ranges by ETag, length and range digest; it does not claim a full-file checksum.
- **H1:** the [builder](../scripts/build_h1_bank.py) likewise uses observed counts on the released 18,080-gene panel, with library-mass weights for bulk controls and cell-count weights for arithmetic CPM controls.
- **Orion:** the [builder](../scripts/build_orion_raw_bank.py) checks raw integer values against original full-library depth metadata. Arithmetic CPM retains that full-library denominator, whereas bulk counts and their batch weights use counts retained on the saved gene axis. Those two heads therefore have distinct normalization definitions even within Orion; this is documented, not an interchangeability claim.
- **CD4:** the bank stores publisher `log2fc`, uncertainty/QC and availability, with no raw count sums. `add_cd4_family` converts the centered LFC through `log1p(50000 * destination_bulk_control * 2**LFC)`. It cannot recover source cell-depth/expression dependence. This is the most direct non-raw approximation in the submitted bulk ensemble; restricted native panels are an additional limitation for the other sources.

The current dispersion transform preserves every realized gene count total and row depth. It therefore cannot correct the underlying bulk mean profile. It can still change the official MSE sampling correction; a scored MSE change must not be mistaken for correction of the mean residual.

## Three falsifiable hypotheses

1. **Full common-response subtraction removes useful shared biology.** Each raw source is centered over its available source targets, excluding each on-target entry when computing the common vector. Source target populations differ. A real shared response can be removed together with nuisance effects. Prediction: restoring part of the raw-source bulk common vector reduces mean-profile residuals on development targets without degrading PDS. Failure of that controlled arm argues against this explanation on that cohort; a general shared offset should not be inferred solely from aggregate MSE.

2. **The bulk response is too small, or its direction is wrong.** Prior shrinkage, cross-source averaging and amplitude 0.3 all reduce some effects. Larger amplitude helps only when their direction transfers. Prediction: development residuals show positive alignment with the predicted response, and the larger bulk-only amplitude improves uncorrected bulk error. If both larger and smaller amplitudes fail, an amplitude-only repair is unsupported. The [previous half-amplitude arm](../reports/h1_all_candidate_comparison.md) worsened raw MSE from 0.975089 to 0.997256, but changed both heads and used the smaller Orion ensemble; it does not isolate this hypothesis for full Model4.

3. **Static source transfer misses destination-specific mean directions.** Raw-source fusion uses fixed weights and availability masks; destination conditioning mainly enters through the destination control profile. Native-panel differences and the approximate CD4 bulk contribution can add context-specific errors. Prediction: persistent mean residuals concentrate in source-disagreement or restricted-panel strata after the two scalar interventions. Inspect those predeclared strata before proposing a separate source-weight experiment. Neither leaderboard scores nor this code review establishes which source should gain weight.

## Registered experiment, implemented with a separate freeze

Use the existing H1 Training development cohort at 400 cells/target, excluding every H1 response source. Keep all source files, arithmetic-CPM desired profiles, donor selection, row depths, pool size, seeds, rounding procedure, and dispersion policy fixed. Do not combine this study with choosing between the current dispersion arms. The four proposed arms are:

| Arm | Raw-source bulk common subtraction | Final bulk amplitude | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Incumbent | 1.0 | 0.3 | Paired reference |
| Partial common response | 0.5 | 0.3 | Test centering only |
| Smaller bulk response | 1.0 | 0.15 | Test amplitude direction |
| Larger bulk response | 1.0 | 0.6 | Test amplitude direction |

The centering arm deliberately holds the CD4 bulk contribution fixed and changes only the raw-source bulk common vector. All arithmetic-CPM head calculations, including their common subtraction, stay fixed. The current builder has a shared `common_subtract` parameter, so changing that flag would contaminate the comparison. A future isolated implementation must expose bulk-only centering and be reviewed before generation. The amplitude arms apply their scalar to the same fused bulk effect. These are separate contrasts against the incumbent, not a Cartesian search or a basis for adding arms after results.

Before generation, record the hashes and diagnostic definitions. Report uncorrected log-bulk squared residuals separately from the official capped sampling correction, plus residual/response alignment and feasible-moment projection error. Evaluate requested and realized profiles: the dual emitter can project the requested bulk profile into bounds imposed by the fixed CPM means and depths, so a requested amplitude is not necessarily the realized amplitude. An arm that exceeds existing feasibility limits fails; do not relax limits after seeing results.

Success requires improved raw normalized MSE supported by lower mean-profile error, with PDS, NMAE, fidelity, reach and Jaccard each nonworse within numerical tolerance (1e-8 on the same raw metric bundle). Also report per-target changes and effective metric target counts. Fixed requested arithmetic means do not guarantee fixed realized means or fixed DE tests after integer emission. Audit their drift, and score all six metrics. PDS cannot be guaranteed invariant when the bulk profile itself changes. If no arm passes, retain the incumbent; do not recast a tradeoff as improvement without affecting other metrics.

Treat this already-observed Training cohort as development evidence. Freeze any surviving recipe before a single independent reserved confirmation; keep the previously closed H1 Test and K562 confirmations closed. A development winner would still require the complete final-context input, generation, validation and submission process; the user has authorized the local experiment and conditional confirmation, while no official submission is part of this stage. No gain is promised.
